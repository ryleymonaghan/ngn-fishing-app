Frequently Asked Questions

How do I generate a fishing report?

Tap "Generate Report" from the home screen, select your date, target species, and bait preferences, then tap Generate. Your custom report with GPS spots, rig setups, and a full day schedule will be ready in about 30 seconds.

How many free reports do I get?

Every account starts with 3 free reports. After that, subscribe to NGN Pro ($9.99/month or $59.99/year) for unlimited reports and Pro map layers.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to your device's Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → NGN Fishing → Cancel Subscription. Your access continues until the end of your current billing period.

How do I restore my purchases on a new device?

Log in with the same account you used to subscribe. Your subscription status syncs automatically. If it doesn't appear, go to Profile → Restore Purchases.

What areas does NGN Fishing cover?

Southeast USA coast — South Carolina, North Carolina, Georgia, and Florida. Inshore flats, nearshore structure, and offshore Gulf Stream fishing.

Can I export spots to my chartplotter?

Yes. Tap "Send to Chartplotter" on any report to download a GPX file compatible with Garmin, Lowrance, Humminbird, and Simrad devices.

How do push notifications work?

Enable weather alerts in your Profile settings. You'll receive push notifications when conditions change significantly — wind shifts, approaching storms, or optimal bite window timing.

How do I delete my account?

Email privacy@ngnfishing.com with your account email address. All personal data will be removed within 30 days.

Report a Bug

Found something broken? Email support@ngnfishing.com with a screenshot and description of what happened. Include your device model and iOS version if possible.
